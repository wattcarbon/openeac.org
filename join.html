Get involved

Subscribe below and we'll add you to the OpenEAC Alliance Google Group, which carries the meeting invites, drafts sent out for comment, and the discussion between meetings. You don't need a Google account.

{% comment %} A link, not Substack's /embed iframe: Substack can answer the embed with a bot check and then refuse to be framed, which leaves a blank box. A new free subscriber triggers Substack's "New free subscriber" email, and that address is then added to the Google Group by hand. This is the join path until the Group takes sign-ups directly. {% endcomment %}
