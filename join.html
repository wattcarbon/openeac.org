Get involved

Subscribe below and we'll add you to the OpenEAC Alliance Google Group, which carries the meeting invites, drafts sent out for comment, and the discussion between meetings. You don't need a Google account.

{% comment %} Substack's subscribe form. It loads from the newsletter's own address, never www.openeac.org/embed: that address belonged to Substack only while it served the custom domain, and is this site now. The link under it is for a browser where the form does not load, since Substack can refuse to be framed. A new free subscriber triggers Substack's "New free subscriber" email, and that address is then added to the Google Group by hand. This is the join path until the Group takes sign-ups directly. {% endcomment %}

Form not showing? Subscribe on Substack.

Membership is open to individuals and organizations; you do not need to represent a company.

Comment on a draft

Open the draft from methods.openeac.org and comment in the document.

Propose a methodology

Write it as a Google Doc open to comment and send it to {{ site.contact_email }}. The Methodologies page describes the five steps to approval.
