# Governance

The OpenEAC Alliance develops and maintains open-source, transparent and scientifically rigorous methodologies for evaluating distributed energy resources, to support the creation and verification of Energy Attribute Certificates (EACs). Its principles are transparency, inclusiveness and accountability.

## How the Alliance is organized

- **Steering Committee**, responsible for strategic oversight: Stephen Suffian and McGee Young (chair).
- **Working groups** develop specific methodologies.
- **Advisory Board** of experts from several sectors.

## Decisions

The Alliance decides one kind of question: whether a methodology is approved, through the five steps on the [Methodologies]({{ '/methodologies/' | relative_url }}) page.
