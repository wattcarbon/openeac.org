Certificates issued under the methodologies are registered on WEATS, WattCarbon's hourly Energy Attribute Certificate registry. Anyone can read and comment on a draft.

The methodologies cover efficiency, electrification, solar, demand response and load shifting. Every method goes through at least 30 days of public comment before approval, and each approved version is archived with its full comment history.

The Alliance meets every quarter.
