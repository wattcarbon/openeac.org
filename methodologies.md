# Methodologies

The OpenEAC Alliance publishes open methodologies for measuring what distributed energy resources do. The current list, with each method's status, is at [methods.openeac.org]({{ site.methods_url }}), together with the Shared Guidelines that every method inherits.

## How a methodology is approved

1. The authors publish a draft as a Google Doc open to public comment.
2. The maintainers list it on methods.openeac.org.
3. A public comment period of at least 30 days opens.
4. The authors revise the methodology from the comments.
5. The final version is published as a dated PDF and archived with its full comment history.

A change to an approved methodology requires notice to all of its reviewers.

## Comment or propose

To comment on a draft, open it from [methods.openeac.org]({{ site.methods_url }}) and comment in the document. To propose a methodology, send a Google Doc open to comment to [{{ site.contact_email }}](mailto:{{ site.contact_email }}). The version history lives in the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/wattcarbon/open-eac-alliance).
